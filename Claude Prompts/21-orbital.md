**The physics is deterministic and single-sourced on purpose.** `OrbitalPhysics.solve()` and

**Mobile: verified as far as this environment allows, not fully proven.** Real synthetic touch
`PointerEvent`s confirm the drag-to-aim math is genuinely delta-based (a finger starting anywhere

375px, clear of the 44/48px minimums. **The portrait rotate-gate itself is not verified for real**
— this environment's browser reports a fine (mouse) pointer even at a 375×812 viewport, so
`matchMedia("(pointer:coarse)")` never flips true here regardless of window size. The gate's logic
is keyed correctly (pointer type, not viewport width), but nothing has proven it fires on an actual
phone.

**No browser-driven test suite is committed yet.** The physics layer is now solid and proven; a

**Fonts are vendored, not hotlinked.** Five woff2 files in `fonts/` (Space Grotesk 400/500/700,

**The physics is deterministic and single-sourced on purpose.** `OrbitalPhysics.solve()` and
`.substep()` are the *same* stepper driving both the dotted aim-preview flight plan and the live
flight after launch — that's what makes the preview an honest prediction rather than a heuristic
sketch, and it's the one thing worth protecting above everything else in this codebase. Six body
types: `planet`/`star`/`rock` (attract, solid — touching one is `CRASH`), `repulse` (negative mass,
pushes away, not solid), `blackhole` (heavy attract, solid, instant `CRASH`), `wormhole` (linked
pair by a shared `link` id; entering one teleports you out the partner at the same speed, optionally
turned by `exitTurn`), `booster` (not solid, adds a velocity kick along `dir` when passed through).
Some bodies orbit (`b.orbit` — a moving centre with a period), resolved at whatever simulated time
`t` the stepper is asking about.

**Persistence is hand-rolled, not `gvb-save.js`.** One key, `orbital_progress_v2`, one entry per
level keyed by `pack.id + "#" + index` (stable across reordering or new packs, since it's never a

schema-drift problem before ever touching `gvb-save.js`. Best attempt-count per level maps to a 1-3
star rating (`starsFor`: ≤1 attempt is 3 stars, ≤3 is 2, else 1).

this repo that can erase real progress either gates it behind a second click/dialog or treats it as
this project's own considered decision; this one doesn't appear to be a decision, just unwritten.
Worth a look — see task list.

**No preview or OG card exists.** The board notice (`data-tags="Puzzle" data-new`) has no

**Mobile input already has real thought behind it, untested on a real device.** Aiming is
