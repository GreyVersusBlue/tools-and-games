A Brief History

Stub page. The matching book chapter goes here — see the porting format in CONTENT-GUIDE.md at the project root.

Historians generally regard the events below as critical and age-defining. Dates prior to about 150 AF on Rues — and everywhere in the world prior to about 1000 AF — are approximate.
