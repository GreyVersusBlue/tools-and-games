## Historical Timeline

Historians generally regard the following events as critical and age-defining. Dates on Rues prior to about 150 AF and everywhere in the world prior to about 1000 AF are approximate. The isolation of Rues and the disruption following the breaking of the Lattice makes exact dating impossible. The dates below are commonly used approximations.

| Year | Major Event |
| --- | --- |
| 124 | The Vargoth Empire proclaims itself a nation and seeks diplomatic relations with other nations. Eltiel falls suddenly without explanation. Mists blanket what was once the Skyways. |
| 123 | The Farthest Star conference, called at Fortune’s Bend to discuss the Vargoth threat, is attacked by the Vargoth. |
| 116-117 | Two major floods in rapid succession sweep Aluvair. |
| 111 | A Diamondback skin is found in T’barris, leading to the elevation of King Y’vain Pelle. |
| 110-120 | Heroes of Old Fortune’s Bend appear throughout the world. |
| 114 | Pestilence wracks Konnigstrava. |
| 113 | Eviar Leonate is elected Crown Prince of Ophrailles |
| 112 | Valarmore is destroyed by the Vargoth invasion. |
| 108 | Valaq in Merrigor is abandoned to the forest. Lasaldi is named 7th city in its place. |
| 81 AW | Evenia Mercurio Pentratheron is crowned Empress of the Mecurian Empire on Rues. |
| 62 AW | Regent Berkeley Evers reveals Nira’s mark and takes his seat on the Compass Rose of Spyndlemere. |
| 0 AW | The Desuetude Pronouncement. Large churches are disbanded in favor of good-works based convocations. |
| 133 AF | Lattice travel is rediscovered |
| 175 AF | The Great Rebalancing. Death, Magic and Purpose are brought into balance and protected from Corruption. |
| 182 AF | The Night of Guardian's Glory. The renegade Zenith Ravvin makes a bid for divinity and is prevented. |
| 1400 AF | Garioch strikes Rues from the world, isolating it and breaking the Lattice |
| 1450 AF | Fate is destroyed. Thinking mortals become purposed and the world is thrown into imbalance. |
