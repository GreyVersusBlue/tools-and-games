## Aeledd

Aeledd is the physical and mortal realm. It is sometimes referred to as "the world" or "the real world" but it is no more real or fundamental than any other realm. However, it is the realm that is most physical and most comprehensible to living, mortal beings. It is also the domain of the incarnation of Magic, sometimes known as Tiesh. The regions, nations and peoples discussed in this book all exist on Aeledd.

## The Shade

Just beyond the threshold of the physical world is the Shade, a shadowy mirror where perception and purpose matter more than logic or flesh. Long ago, the Shade was Fate's realm. With Fate's hand guiding it, the Shade was a place where destiny and purpose were decided. During the era of imbalance, it was a lawless frontier. Now it is drawing closer to the mortal world, and even overlapping in places.

## The Void

The Void is a place of remembrance and death. The Heart of the Void is the home of Death herself, sometimes called Lesh, or the Lady of Moths. The Void is accessible to the living via both the Gate of Moths in Fortune's Bend and via the Bridge, but outside of the rare event of the Bridging, the living can travel the Void only briefly and with great hardship.

## The Divine

Stories tell of a Divine Realm that is home to the Genesori. Aeledd, the Shade and the Void exist separately but interconnected and in balance with one another. The Divine realm is completely separate since the Genesori have chosen not to touch the world directly. Those of great faith or in possession of powerful relics may occasionally call on the power of the Divine, but only the Genesori and their Divine Zeniths may physically travel there. As such, almost nothing of it is known by mortals.

## Noteworthy Beings of the Realms

### The Gargoyles

There are beings known as the Gargoyles. These are not the grim, stone creatures of old tales but wondrous creations of Fate, bound with the substance of the Shade and Aeledd in equal parts. Once thought to be mere myth, their presence in the world has been palpable in recent centuries. Each was entrusted with the task to watch for the gifts of the living that Fate held dear and their names speak of those assets: Lorekeeper, Tuneweaver, Heartbinder, Oathtaker, Lightseer, and Wayfinder. Now they watch the Purposed and have made it their duty to bestow blessings upon those who embodied the same gifts that Fate held dear the most.

Lorekeeper wanders through villages and cities, their ears listening for the retelling of tales of old and the first utterances of new tales that could become local lore. Their eyes watch for those who seek out the old knowledge in book and scroll and share the legends of their people. Tuneweaver, on the other hand, floats through the streets and festivals, listening for melodies that stir the heart and move the soul. Their presence is often marked by the harmonious hum of music and song.

Heartbinder moves among communities, oft unseen but ever present, their focus on those who mend broken bonds and foster unity. Oathtaker is a silent guardian of promises and commitments, recognizing those who uphold their word with unwavering resolve. Lightseer, a beacon of hope, roams the darkest corners of the world, seeking out those who bring light and optimism to others. Way Finder is the guide, they watch for those who plan, get ready and prepare. Honoring those who help others be ready for what will come their way.

### The Celestials

The Celestials are the children of Death and Fate, and they are in general the most powerful denizens of the Shade. Each one has a representation among the stars, a constellation. Each celestial has 4 aspects, each associated with a different season. And each aspect has certain words and themes that represent how they influence the lives of the purposed.

### The Vargoth

The Vargoth are mysterious entities that destroyed Valarmore over a decade ago and are currently attacking the Five Duchies. The Vargoth were widely considered mindless monsters, and so the world was shocked when the Vargoth Empire declared themselves a nation, peer to the others of Aeledd, and expressed a desire to open diplomatic and trade relations. Some nations have opened negotiations with the Vargoth, while others support the Five Duchies and their defense of the Howling Gates. For now, most nations remain divided or neutral, either uncertain how to proceed in this unexpected turn of events or unwilling to engage with either side of a war being fought in faraway lands. But more and more peoples and nations are beginning to understand the threat the Vargoth represent.

The foot soldiers of the Vargoth assaults appear to be those who have fallen in previous attacks, reduced to empty Husks by some unknown means. Other people seemed to have Turned to the Vargoth cause with their minds intact. Alice Stormbow, now the High Herald of the Vargoth Empire was once one of their fiercest opponents. Those who have been where the fighting is the fiercest have reported bloodcurdling shrieks in the distance, but if any have seen the source of these noises, they have not lived to tell the tale. All these facts have led to a great deal of speculation about the true nature of the Vargoth and the means by which they recruit their forces – speculation which has thus far produced very few answers.
