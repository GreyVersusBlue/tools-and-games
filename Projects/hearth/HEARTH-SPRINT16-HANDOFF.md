# Hearth v2 — Sprint 16 handoff ("The island talks to itself")

Deliverable: `Projects/hearth/` (`index.html` + `css/` + `js/`, ~266 KB total, zero deps, runs from `file://`). Sprint 16 is the depth sprint the brief asked for straight out: more to watch minute to minute, more to come back to a decade later. Short term, the island's social life became visible — people stop in the path and talk, the children actually play, and the sky has occasions. Long term, the island's memory grew a spine and a pulse: every finished year takes a name, and the grown stories start turning into **songs**, which live only in the people who carry them, and can die. Save format goes to **v:12** (v:5–v:11 links still load; every new field reads as empty).

## Changelog (5 lines)

1. **Talk you can watch, and news that walks.** Two idle grown-ups now stop mid-path and chat (speech pips, leaning toward whoever they're for), and a fresh chronicle event travels mouth to mouth (`p.heard`, with "had it from" attribution) until a fire night collects it — the teller adds the news to the telling, and it stops being news. Gossipy islanders start more of it, and the card knows what a talker is doing and why.
2. **The children play like children.** Tag between two kids (roles swap at the touch, three rounds, then the grass gets them), ring-around at the swing or the fire ring, a snowman when the snow lies deep — which stands exactly as tall as the snow allows and slumps when it goes — and, once the watcher has ever skipped a stone (`skipN`, saved), the children copy it at the shore with the same plink. Deep snow keeps short-lived footprints behind every walker.
3. **The sky has occasions.** A rainbow when rain breaks into daylight; aurora on cold clear winter nights (a pure function of seed and day, so every device that opens an island agrees; cold-tempered islands see more); shooting stars someone awake sometimes sees and wishes on; dawn mist off the low ground in spring and autumn; heat shimmer in high summer. All presentation-only — zero `R()` draws, nothing saved.
4. **Songs, and the naming of years.** A story that has grown and still keeps being asked for (told 6×) gets a tune put under it by a seed-derived singer; everyone at that fire knows it, later fires teach it to everyone present, children learn it — and when the last living knower dies or sails, the song is **lost**, chronicled with weight ("The story is still told, plainly now."). A returner can bring a lost tune home. And each finished year takes the name the chronicle earns it — "the year the rain broke", "the year of the cradles", "a quiet year" — in the Chronicle headers, the export, the log at the year's turn, and on a person's card ("born … — the year of the fever").
5. **Blood shows.** Newborns inherit a parent's skin in the portrait (`fSk` — the rr() stream is still consumed, so every existing face is pixel-identical), the card shows "grandchild of" one generation up through the living or the dead list, the dead get anniversary visits from partners and children (feeding the sprint-15 `vn` piles), and elders tell the young about specific people under the stones.

**What I'd cut if it were too big:** the heat shimmer and the work-hum note pips (the rainbow and the fire-night singing carry their pillars); never song loss — mortality of culture is the point.

## Decisions made without asking

- **The children's stone-skipping is gated on a saved sim counter (`skipN`), not the hints system.** `hintsDone` lives in localStorage — per-device presentation state — and using it in a sim decision would make the same shared island behave differently on different phones. The island itself now remembers ever having seen it done, which is also the better story.
- **Musicality is seed-derived (`(p.seed>>>4)%5===0`), not an eleventh trait.** A new trait would have re-rolled nobody but confused everybody; this way old links get their singers retroactively and identically, and dreamy/funny stand in when no born singer is at the fire.
- **Songs are prose and pips only — no synthesized tune this sprint.** The house precedent is restraint (an answered prayer is deliberately silent), a real melody system is its own sprint with its own listening pass, and a tune you read about and never hear is more in the island's voice. The hook is obvious if a future sprint wants it: a seed-derived phrase through `note()`, `wayTune`-style, at fire nights only.
- **Year names are re-derived, never saved.** `yearName()` reads the packed-anyway chronicle with a fixed first-match priority table, so it costs the save format nothing and old islands get all their years named the moment they load. The quiet fallback shows in the Chronicle but is never announced and never decorates a card — "a quiet year" is a shrug, not a name.
- **The procession does not break for the trader.** Found by the sprint-14 regression, not by eye (see below): the trade boat's shore-wave could yank the bounds leader mid-rite, dissolving the walk. The wave now excludes `bounds` walkers (as it already excluded builders), on the grounds that an elder mid-ceremony leading children does not go wave at a merchant.
- **"Grandchild of" shows even when the grandparent is also listed as a friend.** The kin term is the more important fact; hiding it behind an incidental friendship read as a bug the harness caught.
- **The snowman follows the snow, not the calendar.** Tied to `snowD`, it can die in a mid-winter thaw and would never stand on green grass; "melts in spring" is what usually happens rather than what is decreed.

## Issues / complications I hit

- **The trader broke the bounds, and had always been able to.** My new `R()` draws shifted the stream enough that the `fourteen` regression's walk day now had the trade boat arrive mid-procession — the leader went to `wave`, the rite dissolved, one stone short. A probe script following the leader step by step found it in minutes. The fix is the game fix above; the regression was the only reason anyone ever knew.
- **A whole conversation can happen inside one `step()`.** The pair spawn adjacent, `goTo` targets the midpoint, and if the partner iterates later in the people loop it arrives, dwells, and exchanges news in the same tick — so the harness's "did I ever glimpse `task==='chat'`" check was false while both pockets already held the news. The durable signals (the `heard` fields and their attribution) are what the test asserts now; same lesson as sprint 15's log-scraping dead end, one layer down.
- **`yearName()` correctly refuses to name the running year — including in tests.** The first `sixteen` draft asked for year 1's name on day 9 and got `null`, which is the function working. The check now runs after the anniversary section, when year 1 is long finished.
- **A caveat, noted rather than fixed: names recycle, and songs live on names.** A child named for a dead knower (the ancestor-naming rule) technically "knows" every song the ancestor knew, and could resurrect a lost one. It is rare (needs the loss and the naming to line up), it is arguably the island being poetic ("born with the tune"), and fixing it would mean packing knower identity beyond names. Left as is, on purpose, and written down here so nobody trips on it unwarned.
