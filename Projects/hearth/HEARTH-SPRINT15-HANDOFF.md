# Hearth v2 — Sprint 15 handoff ("The hill remembers")

Deliverable: `Projects/hearth/` (`index.html` + `css/` + `js/`, ~242 KB total, zero deps, runs from `file://`). Sprint 15 closes the one place in the island's memory system that the lore trilogy (sprints 12–14: things, places, the rite) walked past without touching: the grave hill from sprint 1. A stone on the hill now visibly holds the weight of the people who keep coming back to it, and the walking of the bounds — which already toured every named place once a year — now finishes there, with the elder saying the names in order, oldest first. Save format goes to **v:11** (v:5–v:10 links still load; the new field reads as zero).

## Changelog (5 lines)

1. **No more size ceiling.** The original build prompt's "under ~250 KB" line is gone (`hearth-v2-prompt.md`), by request — it was an arbitrary proxy for load time, and a few extra seconds on a slow connection is a fine trade for room to grow. Nothing here was written to spend the freed budget; the sprint is normal-sized (+2 KB) and the change is a constraint removed, not a target hit.
2. **A grave holds what's left at it.** Every visit to a grave — a gentle islander straightening the stone, a lonely descendant of the dead who has no living family of their own, a dream-prompted trip up the hill, a plain mourning after a fresh death — now leaves a mark (`gr.vn`). The ground shows it, the same trick as the lore-place cairns: a pebble at two visits, a small flower at six, moss softening the stone by twelve. Uncapped past that, on purpose — the point isn't to finish, it's to keep being visited.
3. **The bounds now ends on the hill.** If the island has any graves when the walking of the bounds sets out, the procession's last stop is the hill: the leader touches every stone (all of them, not just one) and says the dead's names aloud, oldest death first — the same "oldest first" rule the named-place tour already uses. Children get a distinct history line for it ("shown who is under every stone"), and the first-ever walking's chronicle entry mentions the hill stop when it happened.
4. **Save v:11** adds one field per grave (`vn`, the visit count) to the existing `gv` tuples. Older links simply read it as zero — a v10 island's graves start their pile counts fresh, exactly like the sprint-14 lore piles did for v9 links.
5. **The mourn path now remembers which grave it was for.** A small plumbing fix underneath change 2: `p.mourn` was being read once to launch the walk and immediately nulled, so by the time the person arrived there was nothing left pointing at which stone they'd come to sit at. It now hands off to `p.vg` (the same field the direct-visit task already used) before clearing, so both paths to a grave — the wander-triggered one and the death-triggered one — count the same way.

**What I'd cut if it were too big:** the moss tier (the pebble and the flower already carry the idea); never the hill stop on the bounds.

## Decisions made without asking

- **The hill stop touches every grave, not one.** The named-place stones are left one at a time, each place its own thing; the hill is different — it's a single visit to the whole of the village's dead, so the procession marking it is a single act that touches every stone, the way saying everyone's name in one breath is one act and not several.
- **Only the leader's arrival counts, same as the named places.** The kids walk to the hill too (so they get the history line and hear the names), but the vn increments and the "says the names" line are gated on `p.bLead`, matching sprint 14's "only the leader leaves stones" call — a five-person procession shouldn't grow a hill five times faster than a solo mourner grows it.
- **The recited name list is capped at four, with a "more before them" tail** for islands with a deep hill — the same instinct as the made-thing per-seed table: specific and readable beats exhaustive and unreadable, and "and N more before them" is itself in the island's voice.
- **Uncapped visit counts, capped visual tiers (top out at 12).** Identical shape to the sprint-14 lore piles for the identical reason: the number stays true for anyone who counts, the drawing stays cheap and legible for everyone who doesn't.
- **The size ceiling's removal is scoped to the quality bar, not an invitation.** `hearth-v2-prompt.md` now says so explicitly; this sprint still shipped at a normal size. If a future sprint wants to spend real bytes on something (a bigger art pass, a system that just needs the room), it can, but "no ceiling" isn't "no discipline."

## Issues / complications I hit

- **The log-scraping test was the sprint's only real dead end.** The forced "says the names" line uses `say(...,true)` so it always fires, but `logEl` keeps only its last 9 `<p>` children (`core.js:243`) and the harness runs a full sim-day between launching the walk and checking — plenty of other people's daily activity to evict one line. Fixed by trusting the grave-touch counts (a durable, already-necessary assertion) instead of the log text; the log-text version is left out rather than kept as a flaky check.
- **`p.mourn` was write-once.** It gets set by several different triggers (a relation's death, a dream) and consumed by a single `sim.js` line that launches the walk and immediately nulls it — before this sprint, nothing downstream needed to know *which* grave a `mourn`-task walker was headed to, so nobody had noticed it was already gone by arrival. Sprint 14's cairn pattern (leave something at the place you actually reached) needed that reference back, which is what surfaced it.
- **Same version-pin trap sprint 13 warned about, sprung on schedule.** `harness.mjs`'s `fourteen`-mode round-trip test asserted `rt.v !== 10` and failed the moment `pack()` moved to v11 — exactly the failure mode the sprint-13 handoff named and told future sprints to grep for. Grepped for `rt.v !==` this time (not just `o.v !==` from the original warning — the pattern shows up under either name) and fixed both the one it caught and the one this sprint's own new test would otherwise have planted.

## Quality-bar leftovers, for sprint 16

- **The recited names are a `say()` line only — never chronicled.** A hill-walk that touches four stones and says four names produces no permanent record of *whose* names, only that the walk happened. If a future sprint wants the chronicle to remember who was named (not just that naming happened), that's a small addition to the existing `bounds` chron entry's `st` text.
- **Grave visit counts don't currently feed anything back** the way a grown story feeds the ground. A stone with 700 visits and a stone with one are only visually different, never narratively — no flavor line yet reads "the most-visited stone on the hill." Cheap texture if wanted, in the same vein as the child who counts the cairn stones (sprint 14's flavor line).
- **The rich-island cap overshoot (pop 49 via births) is still pre-existing and still mild** — the fifth sprint in a row to note it and the fifth to conclude it isn't worth spending a sprint on; the `popCap()+1` allowance in the birth rule remains the number if it ever actually grates.
- **Prayer kinds are still checked in a fixed priority** (heal > rain > food > calm > dream, from sprint 11); untouched again this sprint, noted again for whoever eventually wants to.
- The listening pass stays one command (`node harness.mjs twelve`) — run it whenever a sound is added or a gain touched. Nothing was touched this sprint; it was run anyway and reads clean.
