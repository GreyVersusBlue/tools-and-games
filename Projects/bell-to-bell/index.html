It costs Bandwidth. The lesson does not pause while you use it. And your furniture casts blind spots — the cabinet and the bookshelf hide what is behind them, so some things can only be seen from somewhere else in the room.

Mastery is not a bar you fill. It is the average of what twelve people understand, and each of them has their own number. Deliver from the front strip and those numbers climb. A kid with a phone under the desk is not learning anything, which is what the phone is actually costing you.

Check for understanding to see the comprehension aura in Withitness. It goes stale in about a minute. You are always deciding whether you have time to know.
