Walkthrough Mode

WASD move  ·  mouse look  ·  Shift sprint
Space jump  ·  F ghost mode (no-clip, Space/C to fly)
E ride the elevator you're standing in  ·  P photo mode
B ring the bell  ·  N PA announcement  ·  U sound panel
Esc release mouse

Walls, glass, railings, windows and furniture stop you; doors open as you approach and shut behind you. You start on the floor you were editing — walk onto a staircase or a ramp to climb it, step into an elevator and press E, and you'll stop at the edge of a mezzanine rather than walk off it.

The building has a sound now: your footsteps take their material from the floor finish under them, doors latch and shut, machines hum where they stand, and every room reverberates for as long as its own volume and surfaces say it should. The sound panel (U) shows the number and holds the volume.
