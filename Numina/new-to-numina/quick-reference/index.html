New to Numina

Quick Reference

A cheat sheet of the practical, easy-to-forget stuff. Nothing here needs to be memorized before your first event — bookmark the page and come back to it.

Bookmark these

  • The official Numina site — event dates, pricing, and registration.
  • The Numina Discord — the fastest place to ask a question, borrow gear, arrange a ride, or tell staff you’d like to come NPC.
  • numina.lorelogic.info — the newer game system, handling registration and Post-Event Letters. Numina is mid-migration to this system from an older one, so if a link seems broken or a feature is missing, that is probably why — ask in the database-things Discord channel rather than assuming you did something wrong.

Getting the Discord roles you need

Set your Discord display name to “Real Name/Character Name” (or “Real Name/TBD” if you have not picked one yet), then message a staff member directly and ask to be given the player or NPC role. This is a manual step on staff’s end, not automatic, and it is the single most common thing new players miss — skip it and you will not see channels you need, including the new-player and NPC ones.

What an event costs

The event fee is $125, and two nights in a cabin are included in that. It is worth checking the registration store when you sign up rather than assuming, because the fee has gone up more than once over the years.

Meals are separate, optional, and only offered at Camp Horizons — figure somewhere around $36 for two breakfasts and Saturday dinner. You have to commit to meals in advance; there is a cutoff roughly a week before the event and you cannot buy in at the door. Prince William Forest Park has no meal service at all, so bring a cooler or plan on a twenty to thirty minute drive each way to find food.

Prepaying the whole season in one payment during the early-year window earns half a bonus Character Point. The window is genuinely limited to the start of the year, so ask on Discord when it opens rather than waiting for an announcement to find you.

You can pay by PayPal to ZYZ LLC (@NuminaLarp), or by cash or check at check-in — that works at both sites, whatever you may have heard to the contrary. If you pay in advance, screenshot the receipt before you leave home. There is no cell service at either site, so a receipt you can only reach online is a receipt you do not have.

There is no late fee, and registration stays open right up to game time. The meal cutoff is the only deadline that actually bites.

If money is the thing standing between you and an event, come as an NPC instead — it costs nothing and your meals are covered. See NPCing.

Character Points and Post-Event Letters

You gain Character Points for playing, for NPCing, for submitting a character history, and for supporting the game through volunteering or prop-making. A big part of that comes through your Post-Event Letter (PEL) — a short write-up of what your character did — submitted through the online system after every event you attend, usually due by the Monday night after. Yes, even staff and NPCs write PELs; it is not a player-only mechanic. If the online system is down, email NuminaPEL@gmail.com instead.

Who to email

  • Rules questions — NuminaRules@gmail.com
  • Character history / backstory — NuminaHistory@gmail.com
  • Character sheet changes — NuminaCharacter@gmail.com
  • Post-Event Letters, if the online system isn’t working — NuminaPEL@gmail.com
  • General player concerns — NuminaPlayerRep@gmail.com (this is your Player Representative — a good first stop if something feels off)
  • Serious escalations — NuminaDirector@gmail.com

The two sites

  • Camp Horizons (Harrisonburg, VA) hosts the season-opener and season-closer. Expect a rustic cabin, a real hill between the tavern and the mod sites, and the coldest weather of the season — pack for cold, see What to Pack.
  • Prince William Forest Park hosts the two events in between. It shares its site through a park-wide lottery with other groups, so its exact dates each year are not confirmed until after New Year’s — check the official site rather than assuming a pattern. It runs warmer, has no catering, and does not allow cash payment on-site.

Either way, use the specific campsite address the event gives you for GPS, not just the park’s name — the wrong entrance can cost you a real detour.

A few other things worth knowing

  • Numina expects players to stay reasonably current on vaccinations per CDC guidance, but this runs on the honor system — there is no check-in screening. If a specific event ever requires proof, staff will say so.
  • Numina has an Instagram (@numinalarp) and a Facebook page for event photos and announcements outside Discord.
  • If anything about registration, payment, or where to send a form feels confusing, that is normal — ask in Discord rather than guessing. The community expects new players to ask questions and would much rather clear something up than have you show up unsure.
