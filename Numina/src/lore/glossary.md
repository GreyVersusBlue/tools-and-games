## Accelerant

The live-action roleplaying rules system Numina is built on, covering game
actions, calls, and combat. See [Core Rules](/mechanics/core-rules/).

## Aeledd

The world in which Numina is set. Pronounced EYE-leth.

## The Age of Works

The current age, beginning in the year 0 AW with the Desuetude Pronouncement.
Numina III is set here. Dates before it are recorded as years before the Age
of Works (AF). See [A Brief History](/lore/history/).

## Aspect

One of the elemental identities — Arcane, Beast, Construct, Divine, Ghost,
Elemental, Life, Plant, or Shade — that shapes a character's nature and
grants access to Aspect skills. See [Peoples of Aeledd](/lore/peoples/) and
[Aspects](/mechanics/skills/aspects/).

## Character Points (CP)

The currency spent to purchase skills when building a character. Characters
start with 50 CP and can earn more through play, character history
submissions, and volunteering. See
[Building a Character](/mechanics/building-a-character/).

## Centering

The state a character enters by completing a Short Rest, and loses the moment
they step away from the spot where they took it. Earth Domain skills and the
Excellencies aligned to them are built on it. See
[Adventurer Skills](/mechanics/skills/).

## Convocation

An organized body of faith that grew out of the Desuetude Pronouncement,
replacing the old church hierarchies with congregations devoted to good
works. See [Faith & Religion](/lore/religion/).

## Culture

The nation or people a character calls home, granting access to Culture
skills tied to that nation's traditions. See
[Cultures](/mechanics/skills/cultures/) and
[Nations & Cultures](/lore/nations/).

## The Desuetude Pronouncement

The revelation passed down by the Genesori through their Zeniths that
dismantled the great churches in favor of convocations devoted to bettering
the world. Marks the start of the Age of Works. See
[Faith & Religion](/lore/religion/).

## Domain

A sphere of ability — such as Water, Air, or Fire — that a character can
pursue for advantages in a specific role, without defining everything about
who they are. See [Domains](/mechanics/skills/domains/).

## Excellency

A mark of exceptional capability layered onto a character's skills. Most are
openly available; others are Hidden Excellencies discovered through
roleplay and adventure. See
[Excellencies](/mechanics/skills/excellencies/) and
[Hidden Excellencies and Expressions](/mechanics/skills/hidden-excellencies-expressions/).

## Expression

A way a character expresses their power — through what they focus on, where
they travel, or what they create — chosen at character creation alongside
their Aspect and Culture. See [Expressions](/mechanics/skills/expressions/).

## Foundation

The trade, calling, or way of life — such as Agriculture, Military, or
University — that grounds a character's skills. See
[Peoples of Aeledd](/lore/peoples/) and
[Foundations](/mechanics/skills/foundations/).

## Flurry

The limit on consecutive attacks: three melee swings that make contact before
stepping out of range to reset, and three packets before three seconds of
visible roleplay. See [Core Rules](/mechanics/core-rules/).

## The Genesori

The one divine pantheon of Aeledd. Each Genesori took a piece of creation as
their domain, and their direct intervention in the world is rare enough
that the last time occurred at the end of the War of the Heavens, when
Garioch struck the continent of Rues from the world. See
[Faith & Religion](/lore/religion/).

## Fortune's Bend

The wandering home of heroes and the site of Death's gate — a crossing point
between the worlds of the living and the dead, appearing throughout history
under many names.

## The Lattice

The network that allows Threshold travel between the nations of Aeledd.
Broken when Garioch struck Rues from the world; travel was rediscovered in
133 AF. See [Travel & Commerce](/lore/travel-commerce/).

## Long Rest

Five minutes of rest taken in a Place of Peace. An 'Imbue Long Rest' effect
grants it without the time. See [Adventurer Skills](/mechanics/skills/).

## Packet

A small bean bag filled with bird seed, thrown or touched to a target to
deliver a ranged effect. Packets cannot be Disarmed or Destroyed, and they
are visible in-game. See [Core Rules](/mechanics/core-rules/).

## Place of Peace

An area with no general combat in it — usually the main town — where a Long
Rest can be taken. A tag or a spirit can mark one elsewhere. See
[Adventurer Skills](/mechanics/skills/).

## The Realms

The four planes of existence: Aeledd (the mortal world), the Shade, the
Void, and the Divine — each home to its own noteworthy beings. See
[The Realms](/lore/realms/).

## Short Rest

One minute without using game skills, and what grants Centering. Damage does
not interrupt it unless an active skill is used to resist. See
[Adventurer Skills](/mechanics/skills/).

## Surge

The call that resets a packet flurry through a skill rather than through
roleplay, letting a caster throw more than three packets in a row. See
[Adventurer Skills](/mechanics/skills/).

## Thread Skill

A class of skill that enhances or changes another skill's effect rather
than acting on its own — limited to three Thread Skills applied to any
single skill use. See [Adventurer Skills](/mechanics/skills/).

## Threshold

A fixed site that allows travel across the Lattice between nations. See
[Travel & Commerce](/lore/travel-commerce/).

## Trait

A label on a character, an attack, a defense, or an item that says what
flavor it carries and which defenses answer it. A character's race is always
a trait. See [Core Rules](/mechanics/core-rules/).

## Vargoth Empire

A rising threat that proclaimed itself a nation in 124 AW after destroying
Valarmore and attacking the Farthest Star conference.

## Verbal

The short out-of-game phrase called when an ability is used, in the form
"[Effect] by Trait" — "5 Damage by Fire", "Paralyze by Poison". It must be
finished before a packet or missile leaves the hand, and called even under
Silence. See [Core Rules](/mechanics/core-rules/).

## Vitality

How much damage a character can take before collapsing. At zero they fall
unconscious, stable or unstable depending on what put them down. See
[Core Rules](/mechanics/core-rules/).

## The Zenith, the Chosen, and the Exemplars

The beings who serve the Genesori directly and exact their will upon the
world, despite the Genesori's withdrawal from direct rule after the
Desuetude Pronouncement. See [Faith & Religion](/lore/religion/).
