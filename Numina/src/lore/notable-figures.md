The campaign guide names a great many people across sixteen nations, and they
are scattered across as many pages. This is the index: who someone is in one
line, and where to read more. For terms rather than people, see the
[Glossary](/lore/glossary/).

## The Genesori

domain. See [Faith & Religion](/lore/religion/).

| Genesori | Title |
| --- | --- |
| Bahadur | The Blade of Resolve |
| Istarum | The Holy Defender |
| Jormic | The Omniscient |
| Thiacris | The Host of Mystery |
| Koreel | The Primal Spirit |
| Eilona | Mistress of the Wild |

**Garioch** is the last of them to physically touch the world: the closing blow
of the War of the Heavens, which pushed the continent of
[Rues](/lore/nations/rues/) entirely through it.

## Figures of faith

- **Exemplar Alessa of Bahadur** — headstrong, zealous battlefield healer who
  went where the suffering was rather than where the fighting was. Her following
  became the convocation **Alessa's Hand**, known equally as healers and as
  smugglers who will run any blockade to get food and medicine into a siege.
- **Ayreaux d'Siniste** — an early member of the Revel, and the namesake of
  **The Order of Ayreaux**.
- **Matriarch Phyllida Vondran** — Koreel's Zenith during the Age of Piety, and
  the reason Koreel's divine and mortal Zeniths have shared a body ever since.

## Beings of the Realms

See [The Realms](/lore/realms/).

- **Fate** and **Death** — parents of the Celestials. Fate's destruction is the
  oldest event on the [timeline](/lore/history/).
- **The Gargoyles** — Fate's creations, each watching for a particular gift of
  the living: **Lorekeeper**, **Tuneweaver**, **Heartbinder**, **Oathtaker**,
  **Lightseer**, and **Wayfinder**. The Lorekeeper is held to have a special
  bond with the Salma Archives in
  [L'dahn](/lore/nations/ldahn-linenation/), and the gargoyles' tenets are
  followed as a way of life by the L'Dahn.
- **The Celestials** — forty-eight children of Death and Fate, each with four
  seasonal aspects and a constellation, grouped into the Broken City, the
  Crossroads, the Faraway Isle, and the Killing Fields. They are set out in full
  on [The Realms](/lore/realms/) rather than repeated here.
- **Stovaresk** — the most powerful secular Oracle in Rues, reached by way of
  Symonovsk.

## Rulers and governments

### Ophrailes

The deepest roster in the world. See [Ophrailes](/lore/nations/ophrailes/).

**The Crown Consulate** — Crown Prince **Eviar Leonate**, advised by Consuls
**Kavie Austen** (Legislative), **Avery Wright** (Judicial), **Johann Dunbarton**
(Military), **Jonathan Morrow** (Arcane), **Dorander Marcellus** (Divine),
**Edward Bower** (Commerce), **Adrian** (for the People), and **Stranach
Vitandas** (Foreign).

**The Assembly of Princes** — **Sawyer Leonate**, Crown Consort, for House
Leonate; High Prince **Tyrodes Sarraffet**, of the wealthiest house; **Camden
Gofraid**, a skilled Shadewalker and a distrustful one; **Ameline Hadley**;
**Juan Dunbarton**, former General of the Armies and the only prince with a
standing militia; the Twin Princes **Dhoma** and **Yesta Morrow**, an archmage
and a divine practitioner; **Urul Crenton**, famous for his parties and
politically idle; **Ulysses Lamorral**, who banned fishing in the Great Bonarey
and is investigating the western blight; and **Skyler Dales**, rehabilitating a
diminished house and agitating against Leonate dominance.

**The Assembly of the People** — High Speaker **Smythe**, a merchant, with
Speakers **Noel** and **Roch** (craftsmen), **Auguste** (a mage), and **Renée**
(a farmer).

**The Court of Arbitration** — Justiciars **Balderic**, **Ebbo**, **Sethra**,
**Tescelin**, and **Thegan**.

**The Gift of Jade Guild** — Guildmaster **Dhoma Morrow**, with Archmages
**Mortimer Gange Dett** (Head Examiner), **Hollister See** (Chief Librarian),
**Emilia Thorne** (Healer), and **Zorander Roshin** (Field Study).

### Spyndelmere

See [Spyndelmere](/lore/nations/spyndelmere/).

- **Regent Berkeley Evers** — the longest-serving Regent in the nation's
  history. He bears Nira's mark, lit when he broke the Depthless Goblet at
  twenty-four; sixty years on, it still burns, though age has forced him to cut
  back.
- **Regent Sage Ashfall** — the youngest Regent, bearing Ferris' mark, lit only
  a couple of years ago. Her grandmother **Linden Ashfall** was a Regent before
  her and hoped she would follow.

### L'dahn Linenation

The High Council runs fifteen strong — nine Elders and six Primes. See
[L'dahn Linenation](/lore/nations/ldahn-linenation/).

- Elders **Landry Hale** (Advocate), **Sol Elfrish** (Guardian), and **Arden
  Lockland** (Fixer).
- Primes **Sybil Driffen** (Advocate, year three), **Cedar Wuveth** (Fixer, year
  one), and **Lianie Oshuni** (Guardian, year two), who filled the seat left by
  Clay Raynar's death.
- **Hollis Lowtrow** — served three years as Prime Fixer, stepped down rather
  than take the Trials, and left for T'barris.

### Rues

See [Rues](/lore/nations/rues/).

- **Empress Evenia Mercurio Pentratheron** — took the Mercurian throne at
  twenty-six and has ruled its golden age for forty-two years.
- **Venius Mercurio** — her eldest son, unwed.
- **Aspira** — her second-eldest, married to **Hethryl Mythandros**, second
  princess of the Federation.
- **Ayril Mythandros** — the Shining Radiant Monarch of the Federated Kingdom of
  the Selda'nire.
- **Jackal** — Anbara's heraldric, who accompanies the Unmasked Monarch to
  important functions.

### T'barris

See [T'barris](/lore/nations/tbarris/).

- **King Y'vain Pelle** — a good ruler early on, now increasingly set on
  retiring and increasingly open to outside influence. Eleven years since the
  last Diamondback skin was found.
- **The Council that Will Be** — **Pierce Kenbow** of the Nettle, **Nuane
  Le'tor** of the Azure, **Caul Arix** of the Sloth, **Myra Rimane** of the
  Scaled, and **Wixyra Harper** of the Golden.
- **Ilene Gieger**, of the Council that Was, represents the alchemists.

### Elsewhere

- **Arbiter Ilia Stolarz** — head of state of
  [Dovenost](/lore/nations/dovenost/), young, healthy, and uninterested in
  international diplomacy. Rumored to have won the position in a game of chance.
- **Arion Galanis of Myos** — Monarch of the
  [Myos Islands](/lore/nations/myos-islands/) these four years, and said to be a
  very competent leather worker. **Sofia Kellis** is the Steward of recent note,
  always available to lend a hand or smooth a discussion.
- The **Albescu family** of Chentva in [Merrigor](/lore/nations/merrigor/) — won
  the last cycle of the Great Game, and now stand accused of producing works
  that are literally haunted.
- The **Rappnos** bloodline holds the throne of the
  [Mists of Eltiel](/lore/nations/mists-of-eltiel/), opposed by the **Revenants
  of Rappnos**.

## Founders and legends

- **The Original Compass Rose** — **Ignatia**, **Skylar**, **Nira**, and
  **Ferris**, the four adventurers whose deeds gave
  [Spyndelmere](/lore/nations/spyndelmere/) its prosperity and whose Knight's
  Marks still appear on the worthy.
- **Louis Wenform**, once Louis Tinker — tamed the wilds around Embervale with
  his contraptions, making the city of Thornwen possible, and was granted the
  name for it.
- **Agnes Shadowseeker**, once Agnes Fletcher — killed a shadow-eyed monster in
  the southern forest and lost her brother **Oliver** to its kin. The name
  passed to her family and is still held in high regard.
- **Berolt** and **Elysande** — brother and sister who set out to build their
  legacies together, quarreled, and parted. She founded Valarmore; he founded
  the [Five Duchies](/lore/nations/five-duchies/). They reconciled in old age.
- **Archibald Dahniley** — proposed founder of the L'Dahn way of life, who
  travelled all his long life after his home was destroyed and never found a
  place to settle. **Lester Leshon**, his partner and protector, co-founded the
  way and built Dahnhelm after he died; their adopted daughter **Dihnora
  L'Dahn** expanded its kindness and fairness.
- **Devan Khoarst** — a L'Dahn Guardian of old who founded the combined-form
  fighting school that every L'Dahn is said to learn the basics from.
- **Isidora Delacroix**, "Isidora the Stone" — first leader of the Mellurian
  Leadership Council and architect of its guild system. The Delacroix Cliffs
  carry her name. With her came **Alaric Borges** "the Hammer" (metalworkers'
  guilds, and the thin strong steel that marked early Mellurian swords),
  **Thalia Tauser** "the Prospector" (miners' guilds, and said to smell ore
  underground), **Derrick Addington** "the Wrench" (the first machinists'
  guild; machines never failed around him), and **Agoris Kodel**, first to
  infuse materials with magic — a pendant whose light never faded — and namesake
  of the Kodel College of Alloys. See [Melluria](/lore/nations/melluria/).
- **Carolin Myosine** — the adventuring ship captain who sailed blind through a
  storm, found the Myos Islands empty, and declared herself Monarch and her crew
  Nobles.
- **Honor Dante** — a L'Dahn Fixer whose engineering so improved Melluria's
  mining machinery that the Machinist's Guild invited an outsider to join.
- **Kit Jantith** — a L'Dahn Advocate, known through the Myosi Islands as fair
  but shrewd, and for over-indulging in the parties after a settlement.

## Figures of history

- **Crown Prince Eduard Dales** — the only Crown Prince of Ophrailes ever
  recalled, having nearly caused a civil war two centuries ago. **Prince Alistair
  Leonate** led the recall and founded the Assembly of the People and the
  peasant-protection laws in its wake.
- **King Hadley** — the last king before Ophrailes was Ophrailes; his daughter
  **Pritan** became its first Crown Prince.
- **Leyla Maral** — a civil engineer who won Dovenost's eighteenth War of
  Ascension after a volcano killed most of the other faction leaders, and whose
  real legacy is roads, aqueducts, and sewers.
- **Martin Henrik** and **Ivan Goran** — the last War of Ascension decided by
  single combat. Goran would not yield and Henrik had to take his head; being
  dangerously stubborn has been *Goranesque* ever since.
- **Tomasz Bosko** — won the only bloodless War of Ascension, during a famine
  too severe for anyone to fight, and is reckoned among the worst Arbiters
  Dovenost ever had.
- **Sona Zoric** — a young Dovenosti mage who tried to open a Gate to her
  paramour's house and opened one into a spice shop in Myos Cori instead,
  ending her nation's isolation sixty years ago. **Claudia Corlevaro**, the
  Myosian mage who untangled it, came back with her and a trade delegation, and
  **Arbiter Aleksandra Fiala** granted the Myosians their Gates and docking
  rights.
- **Queen Azari the First** — ended poverty and starvation in Anbara.
- **Galifel Dyneley** — original benefactor of the Atheneum at Valarmore.
- **Margus Heyworth** — the ranger who found the Howling Cliffs and first hiked
  the pass.
- **Olive Molyngton** — first homesteader of the Five Duchies, founder of
  Northfield in Hare Duchy.
- **Captain Easten Fawkes** — founded the Foxfall Mercenary Company.
- **Zephora Peckham** — at 122, the oldest inhabitant the Five Duchies has ever
  had.
- **Clay Raynar** — a L'Dahn Prime Guardian killed in the line of duty at
  Fortune's Bend, days from becoming the first Prime in twenty-five years to
  serve a full five. His aunt **Viatrix** was made ambassador between Fortune's
  Bend and the High Council after his death.
- **Empress Evenia**, **Eviar Leonate**, **Regent Berkeley Evers**, **King
  Y'vain Pelle**, and the renegade Zenith **Ravvin** all appear on the
  [historical timeline](/lore/history/).

## Figures of the Vargoth War

> **Spoilers ahead.** This section covers the war as it has actually been played
> out at events, not just what the campaign guide prints. If you are new, or
> catching up, you may want [The Story So Far](/new-to-numina/story-so-far/)
> instead and to let the rest reach you in game.

The [Vargoth](/lore/realms/) destroyed Valarmore over a decade ago and now press
the [Five Duchies](/lore/nations/five-duchies/) at the Howling Gates. Their foot
soldiers are Husks — the emptied dead of earlier attacks — but some of the
people they take keep their minds, and those are the ones with names.

**The Turned**

- **Alice Stormbow** — once among the Vargoth's fiercest opponents, now High
  Herald of the Vargoth Empire and its chief diplomatic voice. The only one of
  these figures the campaign guide itself names.
- **General Kiernan Jowenys** — the war hero who defended Valarmore and called
  the Farthest Star Council, where he was stabbed. He was later confirmed Turned
  and rose to become the Empire's foremost general; he sent Fortune's Bend a
  surrender ultimatum under his own name.
- **Colonel Fritz Brithoffer**, "the Clockwork Butcher" — Eltiel-born, Turned,
  and mechanically rebuilt, with Fortune's Bend as his particular assignment.
- **Actherion Enruir** — a Scyllinari diplomat who chose to die beside his dying
  sister when Valarmore fell, and was Turned for it. Among the more sympathetic
  of the Empire's servants.
- **Tuga Kohout** — a T'Barrissian among the Turned.

**Others of note**

- **Commander Lesley Longstepper** — the ambassador killed at the Farthest Star
  Council, the attack that began the present war.
- **Malkor** — an Ophralian mage, Atheneum-trained at Valarmore, who twisted the
  ritual that let the Vargoth loose and altered his own Purpose. He is held to
  be seeking godhood by way of the Husks.
- **The Sly Fox** — a morphose of Spyndelmere, who banished every Vargoth from
  that nation in exchange for the whole of Fortune's Bend walking the mushroom
  path and vanishing for a year and a day.
- **The Tyrant** — a Celestial who rules the Barred Commots absolutely, and a
  wartime ally whose four cyclical aspects are set out on
  [The Realms](/lore/realms/).
- **Lady Alyssa Deloach** — an information broker who trades knowledge and
  favours for scrying rights through a person's eyes for a year and a day. She
  has bargained for stranger payments than that. Her manservant **John** handles
  her business in person.
- **The Faustino family**, and **Lizabetta Faustino** in particular — long-
  running antagonists, who sabotaged the original Fortune's Bend node
  specifically so that no heroes could be called to it.
- **Lance** — former head of the mercenary company the Severed Tongue, revealed
  as Turned.
- **Pru** — leads the Unlit Halls, which stayed closed to her for years after a
  betrayal from within. Newcomers need a patron to vouch for them.
- **Valteraxis**, the Bloodgiver — a corrupted Gargoyle bound up with the
  consumption of Purpose.
- **The Forgemother** — a being met in a shared dream, offering the expertise of
  her craft.
- **The Unwanted Syndicate** — **Alestair Karth** the Ashen Quill, the
  **Vulture's Tongue**, the **Hallowed Engineer**, the **Venerable Architect**,
  and the **Guiding Needle**, who work to revive old Shade beings and their
  rituals.
- **Reach Lord Sarah Jackson** — of Hadley's Cove in the
  [Principalities of the Reach](/lore/nations/principalities-of-the-reach/).

Where the world has only rumour, this page has only rumour too. Several of the
figures above are known to Fortune's Bend by reputation, a decoded letter, or a
single encounter, and what is said of them may well be wrong.
