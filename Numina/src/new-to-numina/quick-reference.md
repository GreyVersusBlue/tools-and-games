A cheat sheet of the practical, easy-to-forget stuff. Nothing here needs to be
memorized before your first event — bookmark the page and come back to it.

## Bookmark these

- **[The official Numina site](http://www.numinalarp.com)** — event dates,
  pricing, and registration.
- **[The Numina Discord](https://discord.gg/YUbmyK4Ehb)** — the fastest place
  to ask a question, borrow gear, arrange a ride, or tell staff you'd like to
  come NPC.
- **numina.lorelogic.info** — the newer game system, handling registration
  and Post-Event Letters. Numina is mid-migration to this system from an
  older one, so if a link seems broken or a feature is missing, that is
  probably why — ask in the `database-things` Discord channel rather than
  assuming you did something wrong.

## Getting the Discord roles you need

Set your Discord display name to "Real Name/Character Name" (or
"Real Name/TBD" if you have not picked one yet), then message a staff member
directly and ask to be given the player or NPC role. This is a manual step on
staff's end, not automatic, and it is the single most common thing new
players miss — skip it and you will not see channels you need, including the
new-player and NPC ones.

## What an event costs

Budget roughly $100 per event, which includes two nights of lodging. Meals
are optional and only catered at Camp Horizons; Prince William Forest Park has
no catering, so bring your own food or split a grocery run. Prepaying for all
four events in a season nets a one-time bonus Character Point, but only
during the early-year prepay window — ask on Discord when that opens.
Payment is by PayPal or, at most sites, cash or check at the door — **except**
Prince William Forest Park, which does not allow cash on site because of park
rules, so plan to pay in advance or bring exact cash for check-in there.

## Character Points and Post-Event Letters

You gain Character Points for playing, for NPCing, for submitting a character
history, and for supporting the game through volunteering or prop-making. A
big part of that comes through your **Post-Event Letter (PEL)** — a short
write-up of what your character did — submitted through the online system
after every event you attend, usually due by the Monday night after. Yes,
even staff and NPCs write PELs; it is not a player-only mechanic. If the
online system is down, email **NuminaPEL@gmail.com** instead.

## The two sites

- **Camp Horizons** (Harrisonburg, VA) hosts the season-opener and
  season-closer. Expect an unheated, rustic cabin, a real hill between the
  tavern and the mod sites, and cooler weather — pack accordingly, see
  [What to Pack](/new-to-numina/what-to-pack/).
- **Prince William Forest Park** hosts the two events in between. It shares
  its site through a park-wide lottery with other groups, so its exact dates
  each year are not confirmed until after New Year's — check the official
  site rather than assuming a pattern. It runs warmer, has no catering, and
  does not allow cash payment on-site.

Either way, use the specific campsite address the event gives you for GPS,
not just the park's name — the wrong entrance can cost you a real detour.

## A few other things worth knowing

- Numina expects players to stay reasonably current on vaccinations per CDC
  guidance, but this runs on the honor system — there is no check-in
  screening. If a specific event ever requires proof, staff will say so.
- Numina has an Instagram (**@numinalarp**) and a Facebook page for event
  photos and announcements outside Discord.
- If anything about registration, payment, or where to send a form feels
  confusing, that is normal — ask in Discord rather than guessing. The
  community expects new players to ask questions and would much rather clear
  something up than have you show up unsure.
