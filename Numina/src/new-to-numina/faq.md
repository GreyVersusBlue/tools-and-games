These are the questions that come up again and again, gathered from years of
people asking them. Where another page on this site already answers something
properly, this one gives you the short version and points you there rather than
keeping a second copy that will eventually disagree with the first.

Rules answers here reflect the current rulebook, which was substantially
rewritten in January 2026. If something you read elsewhere contradicts this,
the rulebook and staff win.

## Before your first event

### Is this game 18+?

Yes. Numina is an adults-only game.

### Do I need to know the whole rulebook before I show up?

No, and nobody expects you to. The Accelerant core rules are deliberately small
and everything else is built on top of them. Learn the
[safety calls](/mechanics/etiquette-safety/) and roughly what to do when
something hits you, and let the rest arrive as it comes up. The
[Combat Quick Reference](/mechanics/combat-reference/) is one page and covers
most of what you need in the moment.

### Should I play a character or come as an NPC first?

Either works, and plenty of people do the second. NPCing costs nothing, needs
no costume or character, and shows you the game from the inside. See
[NPCing](/new-to-numina/npcing/).

### What should I bring?

See [What to Pack](/new-to-numina/what-to-pack/). The short version is that if
you would wear it to a Renaissance faire you are fine, and you do not need to
own a weapon before your first event.

### Can I play a villain, or fight other players?

No. Numina III is player-versus-environment: the antagonists are staff's to
provide. Characters with an edge are welcome, criminals are welcome, redemption
arcs are welcome, and villains belong on the NPC side. The full reasoning is on
[Characters](/lore/characters/).

## Signing up and paying

### Where do I register, and is registering the same as paying?

Registration happens at [numina.lorelogic.info](https://numina.lorelogic.info/),
and no — registering and paying are two separate steps. You can be registered
and still owe money. When you pay in advance, put the payment reference into
your registration record so staff can match the two up.

### What does it cost and how do I pay?

See [Quick Reference](/new-to-numina/quick-reference/), which covers the fee,
meals, and payment methods.

### Can I cancel or transfer my registration to a different event?

Usually yes, handled case by case. Ask staff rather than assuming, and ask
early rather than the week of.

### Someone gifted me a registration — how does that work?

Registration can be paid for by someone else, but you still have to register
yourself. If a payment covers more than one person, the note on it needs to name
everyone it is covering, or staff cannot tell who has paid.

### When are next year's dates announced?

Camp Horizons dates are usually known well ahead. Prince William Forest Park
books through a park-wide lottery shared with other groups, so those dates often
are not confirmed until after New Year's. Check the official site rather than
assuming last year's pattern holds.

## Discord and getting help

### How do I get the Discord channels I need?

Set your display name to "Real Name/Character Name", then message a staff member
and ask for the player or NPC role. This is a manual step someone has to do for
you, it is not automatic, and it is the single most commonly missed thing new
players run into. Culture channels are opt-in separately by reacting to a pinned
post.

### Which channel should I ask in?

Roughly: new-player questions in the new-player channel, anything you want a
citable official answer to in the staff-questions channel, and rules
back-and-forth in the rules channel. The rules channel is faster and more
argumentative; the staff channel is slower and authoritative. For anything about
registration or the database, use the database channel.

### I asked staff a question and haven't heard back. Is that normal?

Give it about three days. Staff are volunteers, and the week after an event and
the week before one are both much slower than usual. If it has been longer than
that, asking again is fine and not rude.

### Who do I email for what?

The full routing list is on
[Quick Reference](/new-to-numina/quick-reference/).

## At the event

### What are the cabins like? Can I tent instead?

Cabins are rustic, bunks with camp mattresses, and you bring your own bedding.
No tent camping at either site. See [Day in the Life](/new-to-numina/day-in-the-life/).

### What if I have a medical condition or mobility limits?

Accommodations are handled case by case, so tell staff in advance rather than
hoping it works out on the day. Worth knowing: **there is no professional
medical staff on site.** Bring what you need for your own care, including
enough of any medication for a weekend away from a pharmacy. Medical devices do
not need an in-fiction excuse — nobody will ask you to explain your insulin pump
in character.

If you cannot kneel or get to the ground, ask plot about the standing rest
posture — arms raised, hands behind the head — which does the same job.

### Is there a non-combat option?

Yes, several. Non-combatants are marked with a glow necklace so nobody swings at
them. There are also roles inside a fight that involve no fighting: scouting,
running messages, and hauling the wounded out are all real contributions. See
[Community & Etiquette](/new-to-numina/community-and-etiquette/).

### What kind of packets does my event use?

This differs by site — one uses birdseed and the other uses small pellets. It
matters if you are making your own, so check before you build a batch.

### Will I get my packets back?

Not reliably. Everything on the ground gets swept up together and redistributed.
Do not put your only set of anything into a fight, and if you want a specific
prop back, ask during a lull rather than mid-combat.

### Is there a lost and found?

Yes. Ask at logistics or check-out. Putting your name on your gear makes this
work far better than it otherwise does.

### Can I set up a shop or merchant stall?

Ask staff first. In-game merchanting is a real thing people do, but it needs
clearing in advance, especially if real money changes hands.

### How do I handle something out-of-game mid-scene?

There is no out-of-game state, so the game has tools for this instead: the
safety calls above, and the community habit of stepping to the edge of the scene
in character rather than announcing you are leaving. See
[Day in the Life](/new-to-numina/day-in-the-life/).

## Your character and the database

### Where do I build my character?

Registration and character building currently live in two different systems
while the game migrates between them. Register at
[numina.lorelogic.info](https://numina.lorelogic.info/); if a link seems broken
or a feature seems missing, that is usually the migration rather than something
you did wrong. Ask in the database channel.

### Do I need to send staff my character sheet before my first event?

No. Build it in the database and staff will have it.

### Where does my backstory go, and is it worth anything?

Either upload it to your character's Attachments tab in the database or email it
to the history address on
[Quick Reference](/new-to-numina/quick-reference/) — both reach staff. It is
worth bonus Character Points, so it is one of the cheapest ways to start with a
slightly stronger character.

### My sheet won't let me remove a skill, only add one. What do I do?

Email the character-sheet address and ask to be unlocked. This is reportedly
the single most common request staff get, so you are not doing anything wrong
and you are not bothering anybody.

### Do I get a free rewrite if I hate my build?

You get one free rewrite. Respecs after that are still possible, they are just
not free.

### Do I keep my Character Points through a database or rulebook change?

Yes. Earned CP carries over on top of the starting baseline. Rewrites of the
rulebook have historically come with free respecs for anyone whose skills were
affected, so if a change invalidates something you bought, ask rather than
eating the loss.

## Info skills and research

This is the most-asked mechanic in the game and the one least covered
elsewhere, so it gets a little more room.

### What are info skills, for someone who has never used one?

They are the mechanism for asking questions between events. You submit a
question against a topic you have access to, and you get an answer back before
or during the next event — sometimes as a plain answer, sometimes delivered in
game as a letter or a rumor.

### How many do I get?

Two per event, and that limit is enforced. True Sight sits outside that count
and is limited to one per event on its own.

### Where do my research topics come from?

From your culture, your Foundation, and your crafting skills — not from the
Research skill itself. Buying Research without having any topics gets you
nothing to ask about. If you have no culture, you have no culture topic. Some
skills add a topic of their own.

### I have two cultures. Do I get two submissions?

They are separate submissions, and each one counts against your two.

### Can I send a letter for somebody else, or to an NPC I've never met?

No. Courier requires that you have personally met the recipient, or that the
topic is on your own sheet. You cannot borrow someone else's contacts.

### Can my letters be intercepted in game?

No, unless staff has deliberately built that into a plot. This is a general
rule specifically so nobody has to play paranoid about their own mail.

### What happens if I submit something and then miss the event?

It rolls over at no cost. You do not lose it and you do not get an extra one.

### When do answers come back?

Usually the week of the event, with staff aiming for midweek. Research answers
are impartial and ignore your character's reputation; Courier answers come from
a person, and people have opinions.

### What does "Imbued" mean on an info skill?

It means the answer comes with a mechanical effect attached rather than just
information — you will be handed an effect card along with it.

## Rules questions

Short answers to the ones that come up most. The governing text is in the
[Core Rules](/mechanics/core-rules/), and the one-page version is the
[Combat Quick Reference](/mechanics/combat-reference/).

### Does my shield block spell packets?

No. Shields and weapons block melee and missile attacks, including arrows. A
packet that hits your shield still affects you. Rule of thumb: if the thing
coming at you was launched from a prop, it is a missile and you can block it; if
it came out of a hand, it is a packet and you cannot.

### Can I block with a staff, or another prop that isn't a weapon?

No. If a non-weapon prop gets hit, the hit counts as landing on you.

### What order does damage come off, and does armor stack?

Armor first, then Protection, then Vitality. Armor caps out regardless of how
many sources you have, so stacking beyond the cap does nothing. Protection
cannot be healed or repaired and replaces rather than adds to what you have.

### Which weapon combinations are legal?

This is the most persistent argument in the game, so precisely: a buckler is
held in the hand and never strapped, which means no buckler with a two-hander.
Spear-and-shield is not a legal combination — your options there are spear
alone, spear with a buckler, or a one-handed weapon with a spear. You still need
the underlying skill for whatever combination you are using; a style skill does
not substitute for it.

### Are bucklers and shields "weapons" for skills?

Generally no, which is why skills that enhance weapons do not apply to them, and
why **Destroy Shield** exists as its own call. A shield straps to your arm; a
buckler does not, so a plain Destroy takes a buckler the way it takes a weapon.
Claws are never affected by Destroy Shield.

### Can Thread Skills boost a Grant, or a crafted item?

No. This is the single most common rules misunderstanding in the game. Thread
Skills apply to skills, not to Grants, not to crafted items, and not to effects
that did not come from a skill.

### What counts as a "spell"?

Any packet attack, and any beneficial effect delivered by touch. By default you
need both hands free apart from the packet, though a handful of named skills
explicitly let you cast with a weapon or shield in hand.

### How hard am I allowed to swing?

Lightly. Contact should be a tap. Hard enough to move somebody's shield is too
hard here regardless of what is normal at other games, and you are expected to
show a full swing arc rather than machine-gunning short pokes. See
[Community & Etiquette](/new-to-numina/community-and-etiquette/).

### Are thrown weapons or Nerf darts legal?

Packets and approved foam props only. Nerf darts are not permitted.

### Silence stops my casting — does Maim?

They stop different halves. Silence blocks the spoken component; Maim affects
the physical one.

### I got hit with Double Maim. Which limbs?

A melee or missile strike maims the limb it hit. A packet lets you choose,
unless the verbal names the limb. Effects do not stack on the same limb, and one
Cure clears one Maim.

### How long does a Death Strike take?

Three seconds of uninterrupted contact while counting it out at a normal
speaking pace, against a target who is genuinely unable to move. Step away or
strike the weapon and it is cancelled.

### Can NPCs lie to me?

Yes, most of them. The exceptions are impartial research answers and anything
delivered with "Let Me Clarify", which is an out-of-game guarantee that what
follows is true.
