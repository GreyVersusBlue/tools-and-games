## Expressions

Everyone has somewhere they express themselves. This can range from what they focus on, where they travel to or what they create. When you create your character, you may choose up to two Expressions*: Your first Expression costs 5 CP and the second costs 6 CP.

*In some cases, a player may take a 3rd Expression by giving up their 3rd Excellency slot. If you'd like to do this, please email NuminaRules@gmail.com and staff will help confirm your build.*
