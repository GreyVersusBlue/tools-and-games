Print Packet

Tick the chapters you want and print them as one document with a cover, a combined contents and continuous page numbering — plus the three prebuilt packets and the offline kit.

An event is a weekend in a field, and the rules are six printable pages. A player who wants Core Rules and the Combat Quick Reference and their own Domain has had to print three times and staple. Tick them here instead and print once.

Three packets already made

Each is a link. Send it to somebody and they get the packet, not the instructions for building one.

  • New Player Kit

    Everything somebody coming to their first event needs on paper: how a weekend runs, what to bring, and the two chapters about playing with other people.

    4 chapters.

  • Combat Card

    The calls, the damage types and the states, printed dense enough to fold into a pouch. This one is the Combat Quick Reference page exactly as it already prints, so it is a link to that page rather than an assembly.

    One page, printed as it is.

  • NPC Packet

    For somebody spending a shift in the NPC camp: what NPCing is, the rules that decide a fight, the calls, and the etiquette that holds across both sides of the line.

    4 chapters.

Building a packet needs JavaScript. Without it, every chapter below is a link, and the six chapters marked printable carry their own print button.

The offline kit

The site keeps a copy of itself on the device that opens it: every page, both stylesheets, the fonts and the search loader. Once that copy is on the phone in your pocket, Numina opens in a field with no signal. Nothing is sent anywhere and nothing is stored but the pages themselves.
