Expressions

Everyone has somewhere they express themselves. This can range from what they focus on, where they travel to or what they create. When you create your character, you may choose up to two Expressions*: Your first Expression costs 5 CP and the second costs 6 CP.

In some cases, a player may take a 3rd Expression by giving up their 3rd Excellency slot. If you’d like to do this, please email NuminaRules@gmail.com and staff will help confirm your build.

Aspected

You have not two, but three aspects. This breadth of Aspects allows you additional abilities.

Skill Name Cost Verbal Description Attribute
Third Aspect Included See Description You may choose a 3rd aspect for your character. You also gain one of the following three abilities which can be performed by any of your three Aspect Traits: Bane - Spend an attribute to make a 5 Damage Packet, Melee, or Missile Attack; Balm - Spend an attribute to make a touch packet of heal 5; Repulse – Spend an attribute to make a Packet, Melee, or Missile Attack of Repel 1 Fortitude
Gather Aspect 4 “Heal All by <trait>” Once per Long Rest, when you are presented with a source of any of your aspects (This is a role play decision based on your aspect choice) you can spend 10 seconds of rest at the source and restore all your vitality. N/A
Commune with Aspects 2 N/A Once per event, after taking a Long Rest you can refresh a single 1x / event skill you used. N/A
Aspect Versatility See Description N/A You may purchase an additional 3 abilities from your list of available aspect abilities. N/A

Cultural Expertise

You are studied in your home culture and find strength within by exemplifying that culture.

Skill Name Cost Verbal Description Attribute
Garb Included N/A When you dress in the style of your culture you gain +1 point of value for the armor type you are wearing. N/A

The Ties That Bind 4 N/A 1x / Long Rest you can turn to spirit & go directly to someone you can see who you know to be the same culture as you. 1 Prowess

Empowered Aspect

You are more powerful with your aspect. You can channel additional power from your aspect.

Skill Name Cost Verbal Description Attribute
Channel Aspect Included N/A When you purchase this skill you choose Benign or Malevolent. If you choose Benign, you can convert all Beneficial effects you use to your Chosen Aspect Trait. As an additional effect from this skill, you also gain a +1 to healing or +1 to repair armor effects from Domain skills by your Chosen Aspect Trait. If you choose Malevolent, you can convert all non-beneficial called effects to Chosen Aspect Trait. As an additional effect from this skill, you also gain a +1 to damage effects from your Domain skills by your Chosen Aspect Trait. This is a Thread Skill
Greater Aspect Weapons 4 N/A You gain the Aspect weapon skill of one of your Aspects and your Aspect weapons are immune to Destroy Effects and Disarm Effects. N/A
Greater Aspect Attack 4 ‘6 damage by [Trait]’ When you purchase this skill choose Melee, Missile or Packet attack. Spend one attribute to make an attack of that type for 6 damage by [Chosen Aspect Trait]. Prowess
Greater Aspect Balm 4 “See Description” You can “Absorb to Heal 2”, “Cure” or “Purge” an effect by Your Chosen Aspect Trait. 1 Fortitude

You also gain the ability to save those of your chosen nations. You can use this skill on any who share a culture that you have: Once per event make a touch packet of “Cure Death to Culture” N/A

Hardy Traveler 3 N/A You gain an additional point of one attribute of your choice (Prowess, Fortitude or Insight). N/A

You can look into the future and learn from those visions, preparing for what is to come.

See the Question Included N/A Write your research skill in the following way: I don’t know the exact question I’m looking to ask, but I want to accomplish something plot relevant. Example: Who can I talk to about this thing? Or I’m trying to learn something impactful about <Insert Name> plot? N/A
Speak in Tongues 3 ‘Gibberish’ When you are affected by the silence spell you can still use skills and spells that normally require a verbal. You must say a short phrase in gibberish before you call the effect. N/A
Drinking away the future 4 ‘Grant 2 Protection’ When you are in the tavern drinking with a group of companions of no more than 6 total, you can grant 2 protection to the group. 1 Insight
Blessed of the Constellations 2 ‘1x / Event’ You gain the ability to start a negotiation with Shade beings. If they accept the Negotiation, you can continue to speak and are a spirit until negotiations end. At which point you return to your starting point as a spirit. N/A

You enjoy performing in front of others, be it juggling, dancing or other forms of entertainment.

Set the Scene 4 ‘Grant Defense, Guard’ When you return from an adventure you may sing a short song or tell a short tale to set the scene of what occurred. To protect yourself and your companions for the next set of perils, you gain two ‘Grant Guard’ effects that can be used on yourself or your companions. You may only use this ability once per Long Rest. If you take a Long Rest after returning from an adventure and do not use this ability, it is lost. N/A

Remembrancer

You are tied to Death and help the living remember the dead through their Memento Mori.

Tongue of the Moth Included ‘Speak to Dead’ As with the powers of the moths to hear the voices of the dead, you too can speak and learn from them. You gain the ability to “Speak to Dead” and “Speak to Spirit” of the Dead. This allows you to speak to someone lying dead in front of you, or someone who has turned to spirit after they died. 1x / Short Rest
Carry Token 3 Unique skill based on chosen Momento Mori You may carry one of three tokens on you at any time. You can switch your token after a long rest in a place of Peace. 1. Token of Joy - 1 / Long Rest - if you fall unstable and your deathcount reaches 59 seconds you can stabilize. 2. Token of Sorrow - 1 / Long rest you can increase a melee, missile, or packet attack to be “and Agony by Fear”. This is a thread skill. 3. Token of Wonder - 1 / Long rest, spend two Prowess and “Grant Melee attack Five Damage and Grant Extra Melee attack Five Damage”. N/A
Friend of Death 4 N/A Should you die, you are known to Death as a Remembrancer and may gain additional concessions from Death (or not at their discretion) while you are within death’s realm. Strategist
