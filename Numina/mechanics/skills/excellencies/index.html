Excellencies

Excellencies are the skills your character brings to the game world. A character can choose up to three Excellencies (this is a hard limit).

Excellencies are listed with alignment to one or more Domains. This alignment is informational only and helps you understand the types of skills offered by the Excellency. You may take any Excellency regardless of your chosen Domain.

Grenadier (Fire / Air)

You have a tinkered device that can create different effects based on the ammunition added. Your projector can also be a crossbow and used as such. To use a projector as a crossbow you must purchase the Archery skill. Since a projector is an Archery attack, all attacks from your projector are Missile attacks.

It takes one minute of RP to change ammo types. If your projector is disarmed, you must drop it and cannot use effects while it is dropped. If your projector is destroyed you cannot use the projector again until it is repaired, which can be done for no attributes at a tinkering or arcaneering station. When you assign a given element as your ammunition you gain both skills associated with that element.
